UFO DRIFT: Space Arcade — Privacy Policy

Developer: Bog Vezen
Contact: bogvezen@gmail.com
Updated: 23 November 2026

This policy describes UFO DRIFT: Space Arcade for Android. Version 0.35 uses Google AdMob rewarded and interstitial ad units configured for production, including during internal testing. Registered test devices receive test advertisements. Other test builds may use Google demonstration ad units. Advertising is subject to the age and privacy controls described below.

Local game data

The game saves your best score, unlocked and selected ships, tutorial completion, sound and vibration settings on your device. There are no user accounts, cloud saves or online leaderboards. The Play version disables Android app backup. The separately installed test APK may use Android backup. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may remove local progress.

Age group and privacy choices

The game is intended for users aged 13 and older. It asks for an age group and stores the selected range locally, rather than an exact birth date. This range controls advertising and privacy settings. Users selecting an age below 13 receive no ads. For ages 13–15, UMP receives an under-age-of-consent signal and Google Mobile Ads receives restricted age treatment: personalized advertising and remarketing are disabled, third-party ad vendor requests are restricted, and the advertising identifier is not transmitted with ad requests. This group receives only G-rated ad requests. Users selecting 16–17 receive Google's teen age-restricted ad treatment.

For users aged 13 or older, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks privacy requirements at each app launch. For ages 13–15, the under-age signal suppresses the GDPR consent request. For ages 16 or older, a consent message is displayed when required by Google's configuration and the user's region. Ad requests are allowed only when UMP reports they may be made. You can change your choices through “Ad privacy choices” in Help when that option is required, or through Privacy information → Settings. The age group and privacy check can also be managed there. Google may process device and consent-related information to provide these services.

Google advertising services

Google Mobile Ads may process your IP address (including approximate location inferred from IP), device identifiers, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information. Test advertisements are not a guarantee that SDKs process no information. SDK data is transmitted using encryption in transit. Processing depends on your choices, age treatment, region and Google's services. The game does not request device location permission.

See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can also manage advertising privacy in Android settings.

Score sharing

Share Score creates a score image locally and opens the Android share chooser. No image is automatically uploaded by the game. Data is shared with a receiving app only when you select it, and that service's privacy policy then applies.

Permissions and diagnostics

The app uses internet and network-state access for Google services and optional vibration for gameplay. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or device location permissions. Developer diagnostic menus are disabled in the Play build. Local system logs may be available through Android developer tools; the game does not send its own diagnostic reports to our server.

Retention, deletion and contact

We do not operate an account or gameplay-data server. You can clear local game data in Android app settings. Google's retention and privacy controls are described in its policies. To ask about this game or its privacy practices, email bogvezen@gmail.com.

Website hosting

This policy is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under GitHub's privacy statement. This page has no analytics, advertising scripts or forms.
